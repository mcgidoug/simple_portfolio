About

As a problem-solver at heart, I’m driven by a desire to simplify complex challenges in technology. My background in IT and programming, combined with a strong foundation in customer service and teaching, allows me to approach each project with both a technical mindset and a focus on user experience. My experience as an educator has honed my ability to explain complex concepts clearly, which I apply in my tech work when mentoring others or solving problems.

I thrive in collaborative environments, whether it's tackling new technologies like React and Node.js, adapting to the evolving landscape of cloud computing, or supporting teams in achieving their goals.

Beyond the screen, I’m a lifelong learner, passionate about staying ahead of trends and constantly improving my skills. I’m also an avid gamer and electronic music producer, which keeps me creatively inspired and adds balance to my professional life.
